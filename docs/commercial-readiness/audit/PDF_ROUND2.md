## Current-company impact

None until the owner merges and applies. After merge only: downloads behave as today; every reader can select
rows and download a ZIP. After migration 1: repeat downloads of an unchanged report take under a second instead of
2.5 s (wifi) to 20 s (weak site connection); the first download of each revision is about 1 to 4 s slower.
Storage grows by about 1 GB a month. No inspection, cost, tyre or ERP row is changed.

## Commercial impact

Tenant, country and site isolation are enforced in the database and on the storage path. Approved reports become
fixed, checksummed documents with an audit trail of who generated and downloaded them.
